Thesis Lab: Find the Focus

You do not have to magically “find” a perfect thesis. You can develop one.

A thesis is a rhetorical tool: it helps a writer focus an argument and helps a reader understand where that argument is going. In this lab, ideas become questions, questions become claims, and claims change as the thinking develops.

College writing often uses phrases like too broad, arguable, and so what? without fully explaining what they mean. This lab will decode some of that language along the way.

How it works: On choice screens, select the answer that best moves the writer's thinking forward. When you complete the required move, the Next Thesis Moment button will appear.
