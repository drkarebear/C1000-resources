🩺 Essay Triage

Your draft has arrived. What needs attention first?

You probably can't fix everything at once. Revision gets easier when you stop treating every comment, typo, paragraph, and citation as equally urgent.
🔓 Unwritten Rule: Experienced writers prioritize. They diagnose what would help this particular draft most before polishing everything they see.
