💥 Writers Get Stuck.

The skill is learning how to respond when it happens.

🧰 My “I'm Stuck” Toolkit

Confused →Decode the assignment or ask a specific question.
Blank →Generate questions before trying to generate answers.
Too many ideas →Choose what belongs now and park the rest for later.
Can't find the words →Say what you mean normally first; shape it for the reader later.
Trying for perfect →Write something temporary that gives you material to revise.
Stuck at the beginning →Start somewhere else. Essays do not have to be written in order.
Need information →Stop drafting and read, search, observe, or ask.
Overwhelmed →Shrink the job until the next action is visible.
Behind →Re-enter with one useful action; communication may be part of the process.
