ENGL C1000 · The unwritten rules

💬 Professor Comment Decoder

“Develop.” Cool. What do I actually do?

Instructor comments are often compressed messages: “unclear,” “develop,” “focus,” “awk.,” “so what?” A professor may know what those words mean in context. A student may reasonably see only a mystery.
🔓 Unwritten Rule: Feedback usually needs interpretation before it needs obedience. Look at the comment and the writing around it to decide what your reader may need.

⚙️ Decoder Online

A comment is information. Your job is to interpret it, look at the writing, notice patterns, and make a revision decision.

💬 My Feedback Decoder

LOCATE — Where exactly did my reader have this response?
TRANSLATE — What might the comment mean in this context?
DIAGNOSE — Evidence? Analysis? Focus? Organization? Clarity? Convention?
LOOK FOR PATTERNS — Does this comment or problem repeat?
CHOOSE — What do I want to keep, adapt, add, move, cut, or clarify?
ASK — If I still can't tell what the feedback means, ask a specific question.
