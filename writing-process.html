Writing Process Lab: Behind the Draft

See what writers actually do when writing gets messy.

Writing is not magic. Experienced writers do not always know exactly what they want to say before they begin. They use strategies, get stuck, ask questions, write incomplete drafts, change their minds, get feedback, revise, and try again.

College writing also comes with expectations that are not always explained. This lab makes some of those expectations visible and gives you practical strategies you can use when you are unsure what to do next.
