What to try when you're stuck.

You have work to do. Your brain has other plans. Instead of calling yourself lazy or waiting to magically feel motivated, figure out what kind of stuck you're dealing with and test a strategy.

The goal isn't perfect productivity. It's finding the next useful move.

Rescue 4 · Timer Lab

🍅 Pomodoro Is One Tool — Not a Magic Number

A classic Pomodoro uses about 25 minutes of work followed by a short break. But the useful idea is broader: give the work a boundary.

Choose a work period you'd actually be willing to start.

25 isn't magic. A timer can reduce the feeling that you're committing to an endless task. Test a period that helps you begin without pushing past useful focus.
