⚖️ Make Your Case

Build a claim you can actually support.

ARGUMENT ≠ FIGHT
Academic argument isn't about being the loudest person in the room. It's about making a claim readers can examine and giving them reasons to consider it.
🔓 Unwritten Rule: A stronger argument does not always sound more certain. Sometimes a claim becomes stronger when you make it more precise, acknowledge limits, or account for complexity.

🧪 Claim Stress Test

🎯 Precise?
Can the reader tell exactly what you're claiming?

👥 For whom?
Does the claim overgeneralize?

🗓️ Under what conditions?
What limits matter?

📚 Supportable?
What evidence could reasonably support it?

⚖️ Arguable?
Could a reasonable reader question or complicate it?

🧩 Nuanced?
Is reality more complicated than the first version suggests?

⚖️ Case Made

A strong academic claim is one you can support, explain, qualify, and examine.

🧪 Stress-Test My Claim

A working claim is allowed to change. The point of this test is to find the next useful revision—not to prove that the sentence is perfect.

🧰 Quick Claim Check

What exactly am I claiming?
For whom or under what conditions?
Why might a reasonable reader care or question it?
What evidence could support it?
What would make it more accurate—not merely louder?
What do I want my reader to understand after considering my reasons?
