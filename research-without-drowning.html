🌊 Research Without Drowning

You do not have to read the entire internet.

Research question: ...wait. What was I researching?

🔓 Unwritten Rule: Research is not “find sources, then write.” Writers search, skim, learn, change direction, keep some sources, release others, write, and search again when the draft reveals a gap.

🛟 Research Lifeguard

What am I trying to find out?

What job does a source need to do?

Can I skim before committing?

Does this actually help my question?

Do I know enough to write something?

🏄 You Are Steering the Research

The goal was never to find the whole internet. It was to make useful research decisions.

🛟 Research Rescue

🌊 Topic enormous?
Turn the topic into something you are trying to find out.
🔎 Search terrible?
Identify key concepts, populations, and useful synonyms. Revise the search.
📚 Too many sources?
Give them jobs. Release duplicates and sources that do not serve the project.
😵 Source overwhelming?
Skim its structure before deciding what you actually need to read closely.
🐇 Rabbit hole?
Return to the research question: does this help answer it?
🧠 Sources disagree?
Let evidence complicate or change the working claim.
📝 Can't stop researching?
Write something. The draft can reveal what information is still missing.
🔗 Keeping a source?
Record the source information now—and note why you saved it.
