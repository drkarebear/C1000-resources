ENGL C1000 · Development & Revision

📈 Develop, Don't Pad

Make the idea bigger—not just the paper longer.

PROFESSOR COMMENT: “Develop this idea.”

STUDENT: “Okay... but HOW?”

Words like develop, expand, go deeper, explain, be specific, and so what? can sound like instructions even when they don't tell you what move to make.

🔓 Unwritten Rule: “Develop” is not one specific revision move. Usually, it means your reader needs something more before they can fully understand, believe, or appreciate the point.
Length is something we can count. Development is something a reader can experience.

📈 Development Controls

❓ WHY?
Why is it true/important?

⚙️ HOW?
How does it happen/work?

🔍 SHOW ME
Give a specific example.

📚 SUPPORT IT
Add evidence when needed.

🔗 CONNECT IT
Link it to the larger claim.

🧩 COMPLICATE IT
Add conditions, limits, consequences, or another perspective.

🧠 Development Detected

How can I make this longer?
What does my reader need next?

📈 Optional: Try It on Your Own Draft

👀 The Curious Reader Test

After an important sentence, imagine a curious reader asking:

Why?How?Can you show me?What does that show?Why does it matter?Is it always true?

You do not need to answer every question. Answer the ones your reader genuinely needs.
