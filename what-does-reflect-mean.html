ENGL C1000 · Reflection & Writing Process

🪞 What Does “Reflect” Mean?

It’s more than “I liked it” or “I learned a lot.”

ASSIGNMENT SAYS: “Reflect on your writing process.”

STUDENT: “Okay... so do I just say how I felt?”

Reflection is another college word that can sound obvious until you actually have to do it.

🔓 Unwritten Rule: Reflection means looking back at an experience, choice, process, or piece of learning and figuring out what you understand now because you examined it.
Reflection is not proving that you learned. It is examining your learning closely enough to understand it.

🪞 Reflection Moves

🔙 BEFORE
What did I think, expect, or do at first?

👀 NOTICE
What happened? What stood out?

🧠 INTERPRET
What do I understand about it now?

🔄 CHANGE
Did my thinking, strategy, or process shift?

➡️ TRANSFER
What might I carry into another situation?

You do not need all five every time.

🪞 Reflection Detected

Reflection is not a performance of learning. It is a way to notice what your experience now teaches you.

🧭 Help Me Go Deeper

🧰 Quick Reflection Check

What did I expect or do at first?
What actually happened?
What do I understand now that I did not understand before?
What worked, failed, surprised, confused, or became clearer?
Why does that matter?
What might I do differently—or intentionally repeat—next time?
