ENGL C1000 · Peer review without mind reading

Peer Review Lab: Be a Useful Reader

Your job is not to fix someone else's paper. Your job is to give the writer useful information about your experience as a reader.

College writing has unwritten rules. Peer review has some too. This lab makes them visible: what to notice, what to say, what you do not need to know, and how to help without taking control of someone else's writing.
