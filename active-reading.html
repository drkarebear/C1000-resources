Active Reading Lab: Talk to the Text

Active reading is not about reading faster or understanding everything the first time. It is about noticing what your brain is doing while you read—and using strategies that help you understand.

Think of active reading as a toolbox. Previewing, asking questions, making connections, annotating, rereading, summarizing, using audio or text-to-speech, and taking breaks are all tools. You do not have to use every tool every time.
