Explore Your Teaching

These short interactive labs are for you—not for your students. Each one starts with a familiar teaching problem, lets you test different choices, and invites you to look at the situation from another angle.

There is no score and no single “correct teacher” to become. Take what is useful, question what is not, and leave with one small move you might want to try.

Raccoon philosophy:
Curiosity before judgment. Also, apparently, rummaging through assignment prompts.

A small-lab philosophy

These are not quizzes about whether you know the “right” pedagogy. They are tiny teaching simulations: choose, notice, reconsider, and borrow what is useful.

Start with the problem.Real classroom situations come before terminology or theory.
See more than one angle.Reasonable teaching choices can have different effects depending on the student and situation.
Take one thing with you.Every lab ends with a small “Try This Tomorrow” move rather than a long implementation list.
