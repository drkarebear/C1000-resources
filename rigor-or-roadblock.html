🚧 Rigor or Roadblock?

Where does the rigor actually live? Work through six common teaching situations and separate productive intellectual challenge from obstacles that make the work harder to reach.

Raccoon Check 🦝

Learn Why This Works

This lab is built around a simple distinction: support can change the route into difficult work without changing the intellectual work itself.

Why focus on scaffolding?

Research on adult digital learning emphasizes focused lessons, visual supports, accessible resources, learner–instructor communication, and peer interaction as forms of support that can help learners progress toward stronger independence.

Why include online-course friction?

Online learning requires learners to manage more of the learning process themselves. Course design can either make that self-regulation more workable or add avoidable navigation, access, and cognitive-load barriers.

What is the practical test?

Ask: “If I remove this difficulty, have I reduced the level of thinking students must do?” If the answer is no, you may be looking at a roadblock rather than rigor.
