College Writer Lab

Writing gets complicated sometimes. You do not need to work through these tools in order. Start with the problem you are trying to solve right now. Each activity is designed to help you make a useful move in your actual reading, writing, research, or revision.

I Have a Draft. Now What?

Revision is more than correcting errors. Use these tools to diagnose what your draft needs and make higher-impact changes.

Essay Triage Decide which problems matter most so you do not try to fix everything at once. Open activity → Reverse Outline Map what your completed draft is actually doing and find places where the structure needs attention. Open activity → Professor Comment Decoder Translate instructor feedback into specific revision moves you can actually make. Open activity → Peer Review Give and use feedback that helps a writer make decisions rather than simply pointing out mistakes. You do not have to fix everything today.

Choose one problem, make one useful move, and then decide what you need next. Writing develops through decisions, practice, feedback, and revision—not by getting everything right on the first try.
