Not remediation.
More visibility.

College readers and writers do many things that are easy to leave invisible: figuring out what a prompt is really asking, deciding what matters in a reading, moving from evidence to reasoning, knowing when to ask for help, recovering after a setback, and recognizing when a draft is good enough to keep moving. These mini-labs make those processes easier to notice, practice, and use.

Small tools. Real college work.

The collection is designed to support students without lowering the intellectual work of ENGL C1000.

Make the hidden move visible.

Break complex reading and writing processes into decisions students can see and practice.

Start from the problem.

Help students find a tool by what they are experiencing, not by whether they already know academic terminology.

Keep agency with the learner.

Offer strategies, choices, and opportunities to test what works rather than prescribing one “right” way to learn or write.
