🆘 Who Do I Ask?

The College Writing Help Desk

College gives you a lot of people who can help. It does not always explain who does what—or when you're “allowed” to ask.

🚨 HELP REQUEST INCOMING

“I know I need help with this paper. I just don't know who to ask.”

🔓 Unwritten Rule: Knowing where to get help and what to ask is a college skill. You do not have to wait until you have a finished draft—or until things are going badly.

☎️ Help Desk Directory

👩‍🏫 Professor
Assignment expectations, course-specific questions, feedback, grades.

✍️ Tutor
Ideas, drafts, organization, revision, reading as a reader.

📚 Librarian
Search terms, databases, source types, research strategies.

👥 Classmate
Reader reaction, brainstorming, shared course context.

💻 Tech Help
Access, logins, platforms, technical problems.

More than one route can be useful.

🎉 Help Desk Navigator Unlocked

Asking for help is not one skill. Matching the problem to a useful person—and giving them enough information to help—is the skill.
