C1000 / C1000E Reading Lab

🎯 Read Like You Have a Purpose

You do not annotate a reading. You annotate for something.

The goal: stop highlighting everything and start marking what actually helps.

Step 1 of 6 The highlight trap
Step 1 · The Highlight Trap

When Everything Looks Important

Classic problem: You start highlighting. Important. Important. Definitely important. Three pages later, almost everything is yellow.

The issue is not that you are bad at annotation. The issue is that you started without a mission.

What should you look at first when you know a reading will be used for an assignment?

Reading rule: Knowing what you will eventually have to do changes what you notice while you read.
Step 2 · Mission 1

Find the Author's Position

🎯 Your mission: Tomorrow you need to explain what the author believes about phones in college classrooms.
Annotation budget: 123 three highlights only.
Step 3 · Same Reading, New Mission

Find Evidence You Could Actually Use

🎯 Essay question: Should colleges limit phone use in classrooms?

This time, choose two details that could actually help you write about that question.

Key idea: Something can be important to the reading without being useful for your particular writing task.
Step 4 · Mission 3

Find Something Worth Questioning

🎯 Your mission: Find a place where you could question, complicate, or push against the author.
Understand the argument Claim + reason + qualification
Find usable evidence Details that help answer your essay question
Find something to question Assumptions, complications, missing perspectives, tensions
Same reading. Different maps. There is no single correct way to annotate a text. What you mark depends on what you plan to do with it.
Step 5 · Highlight Crime Scene

Save the Annotation

Someone highlighted almost everything. Your job: keep only the parts that help with the mission.

🎯 Mission: Tomorrow you must explain the author's main argument. Keep only three highlights.

Which margin note will Future You understand?

🎯 TARGETDirectly helps with my assignment.
💡 IDEAImportant to the author's argument.
❓ QUESTIONI do not understand this, or I want to challenge it.
🔗 CONNECTIONConnects to another idea, source, experience, or part of my essay.
Step 6 · Your Reading Mission

Decide What You Are Hunting For

What will you need from the reading? Choose up to three.

    My rule: If I cannot explain why I am marking it, I probably do not need to mark it yet.
